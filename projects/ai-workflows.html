AI-Assisted Accounting Workflows

Accounting Operations Intern, CareRite Services, LLC | Jun 2026 to Aug 2026

Overview

During my internship with a healthcare organization managing $500M+ in assets across 100+ medical facilities, I supported the month-end close and built AI-assisted workflows to make routine accounting tasks faster.

What I did

  • Completed 5 account reconciliations covering bank balances, prepaid insurance, and accruals.
  • Reviewed ledger data in Excel and Sage Intacct, prepared comparison schedules, and identified discrepancies.
  • Reviewed general ledger and balance sheet accounts to support financial reporting and audit activities.
  • Developed workflows using Claude and Cursor for financial data collection, spreadsheet creation, and reporting.

Results

  • Guided an 8-person accounting team on reusable prompts and automations.
  • The automations remained in use after my internship ended.

Tools

Claude, Cursor, Microsoft Excel, Sage Intacct
